Questions & roadmap

The web app is the product today. Mobile browsers already work.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — open this site in Safari or Chrome on your phone. Dedicated iOS and Android apps come later; a browser install (PWA) is on the roadmap. You do not need a native app to read or ask a preview question.

What is free vs tester vs Pro?

Anyone can read the Bible at /read and ask one preview question here. Tester is invite-only full AI chat for 14 days (no card). Pro is the public path: 7-day trial, then $4.99/mo.

How do I get tester access?

You need an invite code. There is no public tester signup. If you already have a code, use the tester form above. Returning testers log in at the top.

What does “John” mean?

John is the default wake word for hands-free study in the app. The homepage preview is just “Ask a free question.” You can change the wake word in Settings after you sign in.

Live sources when a reference resolves: English plus SBL Greek NT or Westminster Leningrad Codex Hebrew from bible.helloao.org. Byzantine or Textus Receptus when you ask. Spoken answers use transliteration; tap Sources under a reply to verify the fetched text.
