# Billing policy for past-due access

Internal policy. History: on 25 October 2026 Spencer decided that past due customers are limited. On 26 October 2026 Spencer decided that a customer who already has a paid account keeps it when the payment problem is on Whop's end. This document describes the behavior merged to main as 0b590b8684544ca64bd4fab2d7a17af0908a1d92 by PR #42 (https://github.com/SAW72/word-in-context/pull/42). That change updates the app and the server; it does not change the email text or the public terms.

## 1. Past due customers

Spencer decided on 26 October 2026: "If they already have a paid account they should keep if it's on whops end." This replaces the 25 October rule that past due customers are limited, for Whop. The behavior below is merged to main as 0b590b8684544ca64bd4fab2d7a17af0908a1d92 by PR #42 (https://github.com/SAW72/word-in-context/pull/42). Line numbers below are on main 0b590b8684544ca64bd4fab2d7a17af0908a1d92 (0b590b86).

One rule decides past due access for the app (requireAuth, server.js:326 and server.js:351), password login (the route at server.js:1675, its access check at server.js:1692) and the magic link (userHasAccess, server.js:677 and server.js:683). The rule is pastDueAccess (lib/security.js:497 through lib/security.js:509). Chat uses requireAuth (server.js:2995). POST /api/share-tts and POST /api/share-bg-image use userHasAccess when their own sign in setting is on (server.js:1975 and server.js:2204), so they follow the login result. When they refuse someone, the status is 401 with their own text (server.js:1976 and server.js:2205). A refused app request gets 403 with "Subscription required or trial expired." (server.js:353).

### Whop past due: keeps access while Whop retries

A Whop past due customer keeps full access to the app, password login and the magic link for up to 7 days from whop_event_at while Whop retries the payment. All of these must be true (pastDueAccess, lib/security.js:497 on 0b590b86):

1. access_granted is set.
2. status is past_due. A Whop payment.failed event sets it only for a renewal (billing_reason subscription_cycle) of the account's own stored membership that is not stale (whopPaymentFailedMarksPastDue, lib/security.js:525 through lib/security.js:526, and handleWhopPaymentFailed, server.js:2604 on 0b590b86).
3. whop_status is past_due. The same payment.failed event records it when the event carries a usable time and names the account's own membership (whopShadowFromEvent, lib/security.js:394, and whopShadowUpdate, lib/security.js:420 and lib/security.js:425 through lib/security.js:426 on 0b590b86).
4. The account has no Stripe customer id and no Stripe subscription id on file.
5. The account has a stored Whop membership id, and now is at most 7 days after whop_event_at (lib/security.js:483 and lib/security.js:501 through lib/security.js:504 on 0b590b86). The cap is checked on every request.

A stale event (older than whop_event_at) or an event for a different membership is skipped and does not mark the account past_due (whopShadowUpdate, lib/security.js:423 and lib/security.js:425 through lib/security.js:426 on 0b590b86).

No other Whop payment.failed marks the account past_due either. On main 0b590b86, past_due is set only for a subscription_cycle renewal of the account's own stored membership that is not stale (whopPaymentFailedMarksPastDue, lib/security.js:525 through lib/security.js:526, and whopPaymentIsForRow, lib/security.js:517 through lib/security.js:522; the write is in handleWhopPaymentFailed, server.js:2603 through server.js:2604). A Whop payment.failed with another billing_reason or a null one, a stale event, an event for a different membership, or an event whose Whop status update failed leaves the row as it is (server.js:2606 through server.js:2607). An active row stays active with full access and no 7 day cap. The server logs `payment.failed left ${email} unchanged (not a renewal of its stored membership, or stale)` (server.js:2607) and still sends the payment failed email, whose subject begins "Action needed" (server.js:2610 through server.js:2611), because the email is sent after the past_due check whatever its result. The email is not sent for a free forever account (server.js:2601), a payload with no email (server.js:2593 through server.js:2597) or a plan not in WHOP_PLAN_IDS (server.js:2591); handleWhopPaymentFailed returns before it in those cases. This is held as section 3 item 8.

A Whop payment.succeeded for the account's own membership clears past_due back to active and leaves access_granted as it is (clearWhopPastDue, server.js:2830 through server.js:2834 on 0b590b86). The clear does not run in these cases (whopPaymentSucceededClearsPastDue, lib/security.js:533 through lib/security.js:537): there is no Whop status update for the event, because the event has no email or its plan is not allowed (whopShadowEmail, server.js:2785 through server.js:2789), the account did not exist before this event, so the row was created by it (server.js:2864 through server.js:2865), or the update failed (recordWhopShadow, server.js:2818 through server.js:2821); the event has no usable time (lib/security.js:535); the event is stale or names another membership than the stored one (whopPaymentIsForRow, lib/security.js:519 through lib/security.js:521); or the row is not past_due or is free forever (lib/security.js:534). The event then falls through to the other payment.succeeded paths in the webhook handler (server.js:2873 through server.js:2894). An event that carries a membership goes to activateWhopMembership (server.js:2876). For a status that maps to trialing or active, that sets access_granted to 1 (and the status, on an account that is not free forever) and, on an account that is not free forever, also sends a login email (server.js:2540 through server.js:2554); production impact UNCONFIRMED. For a plan not allowed it returns before any write or email (server.js:2478 through server.js:2483).

In the Whop retry case, access ends when the 7 day cap runs out. The cap is checked on every request, so it ends a past_due row's access even when no further Whop event arrives (lib/security.js:503 through lib/security.js:504 on 0b590b86). Access also ends when Whop sends membership.deactivated, membership.went_invalid or membership.canceled: the webhook handler applies the membership as canceled, which sets access_granted to 0 (server.js:2869 through server.js:2870, then activateWhopMembership, server.js:2493 through server.js:2494 and server.js:2523 through server.js:2531). A cancel sent only as membership.updated is ignored: that type is not in the handled list or the Whop status list, so the row and its Whop status columns stay as they are and the server logs `ignored event type=${type}` (WHOP_SHADOW_EVENTS, server.js:2773 through server.js:2777, and the webhook handler, server.js:2899 through server.js:2900). UNCONFIRMED: whether Whop in production sends some cancels only as membership.updated. Changing this behavior belongs to a separate Whop webhook PR, not this document.

KNOWN GAP, pending a decision: a Whop membership event that itself reports past_due or unresolved maps to canceled and clears access (mapWhopStatusToUserStatus, server.js:659 through server.js:663 on 0b590b86), even while Whop may still be retrying. The code is unchanged.

For a row whose status is already past_due, any unclear Whop record fails closed and the customer is limited: no Whop status recorded yet (including accounts last updated before Whop status was recorded), an unknown status, an event with no usable time, or a different membership (whopShadowUpdate, lib/security.js:421 and lib/security.js:429, and pastDueAccess, lib/security.js:498 through lib/security.js:508 on 0b590b86). An unclear payment.failed on a row that is not past_due does not limit it (see above).

### Stripe only past due: unchanged

A Stripe only past due customer has a Stripe id, no Whop membership or member id, and no Whop status. Nothing changes for them. Password login and the magic link still issue a token (lib/security.js:507 and server.js:683). The app still refuses them with "Subscription required or trial expired." because requireAuth accepts only the Whop case (server.js:347 and server.js:351). This mismatch is an open item for Spencer.

### Free forever (manual_free): never past due, never downgraded, no billing mail

A free forever account is never marked past due, is never downgraded by a billing event, and gets no billing mail. On main 0b590b86:

1. payment.failed returns before it writes status or sends the payment failed email (handleWhopPaymentFailed, server.js:2601). A Stripe subscription update skips the account (the Stripe webhook handler, server.js:2755). A Whop activation keeps its status and trial end (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2512 through server.js:2513).
2. A Whop deactivation, cancel or invalid membership leaves the account unchanged (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2501). A Stripe checkout leaves it unchanged (activateStripeCheckoutSession, server.js:2639). An email only payment.succeeded leaves it unchanged (the Whop webhook handler, server.js:2883).
3. No trial ending reminder (handleWhopTrialEndingSoon, server.js:2570), no Whop login email on activation (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2540), no payment failed email (handleWhopPaymentFailed, server.js:2601), and no tester go paid email (isBillingFreeTester, lib/tester-expiry.js:18).
4. Free forever keeps access whatever its status says, but only while access_granted is 1: requireAuth refuses an account with access_granted 0 before it looks at free forever (server.js:340 through server.js:341, then server.js:345), and userHasAccess requires access_granted (server.js:683 through server.js:684). The past due build does not change this.

An admin changes a free forever account with Remove free or by turning access off (POST /api/admin/set-access, server.js:2407 through server.js:2428; the write is server.js:2420 through server.js:2421). A Whop event can also change it. A Whop activation, or a payment.succeeded that carries a membership (the webhook handler, server.js:2873 through server.js:2876), whose status maps to trialing or active sets access_granted to 1 and writes the Whop membership id and member id when the event carries them (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2512 through server.js:2520). So the next such Whop event turns access back on after an admin turned it off. Status, trial end and the free forever flag stay as they are. A missing or unrecognized membership status maps to active (mapWhopStatusToUserStatus, server.js:663). A Whop event of a type in the Whop status list (WHOP_SHADOW_EVENTS, server.js:2773 through server.js:2777) updates the account's Whop status columns (whop_status, whop_trial_end, whop_event_at) only in two cases: the account already existed and the event has an email and an allowed plan (whopShadowEmail, server.js:2784 through server.js:2790, used in the webhook handler at server.js:2863 through server.js:2865), or the account was created by this event (server.js:2902). The write is in applyWhopShadow (server.js:2806 through server.js:2807). Even then it is skipped as stale or as a payment.failed on an account with no stored membership (whopShadowUpdate, lib/security.js:419 and lib/security.js:423).

Older paths can also undo an admin revoke. This is code that already exists on main 0b590b86, and its production impact is UNCONFIRMED. On an account that is not free forever, an email only payment.succeeded (no membership id, status, user or member in the payload, the webhook handler, server.js:2874 through server.js:2875) sets status active and access_granted to 1 (server.js:2885 through server.js:2887). On a free forever account it leaves the row unchanged (server.js:2883 through server.js:2884). A payment.succeeded that carries a membership goes through activateWhopMembership (server.js:2873 through server.js:2876). On an account that is not free forever, that writes the mapped status and sets access_granted to 1 for trialing or active (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2523 through server.js:2531); on a free forever account it makes the write above. Clearing past_due for the account's own membership leaves an admin revoke in place (clearWhopPastDue, server.js:2830 through server.js:2837, reached at server.js:2871). It does not run when there is no Whop status update for the event (no email, a plan not allowed, a failed update, or a row created by this event), when the event has no usable time, is stale or names another membership, or when the row is not past_due (see above); the event then falls through to the paths above, and one that carries a membership goes to activateWhopMembership, which for trialing or active on an account that is not free forever also sends a login email (production impact UNCONFIRMED). Other payment.succeeded paths also do not turn access back on: an email only event for a plan not in WHOP_PLAN_IDS (server.js:2879 through server.js:2880), with no email (server.js:2891 through server.js:2892) or for an unknown email (server.js:2888 through server.js:2889), which has no row to change; a membership event for a plan not in WHOP_PLAN_IDS or with no email (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2478 through server.js:2483 and server.js:2486 through server.js:2491); and a membership event whose status maps to canceled (activateWhopMembership, server.js:2493 through server.js:2494), which writes access_granted 0 on an account that is not free forever (server.js:2523 through server.js:2531) and leaves a free forever account unchanged (server.js:2501).

### Stripe to Whop movers: not yet covered

A customer who moved from Stripe to Whop and still has a Stripe id on file is not yet covered. While past due on Whop, they are limited the same way as today. A fix is an open item for Spencer.

## 2. Cause of a failed payment

If the customer's card caused the failure (a decline, an expired card, or insufficient funds), the customer needs to update their payment method.

What happens to access depends on the case in section 1:

1. Whop retry case (section 1, all five conditions true): the customer keeps access for up to 7 days from whop_event_at while Whop retries. Access ends after the 7 days, or when Whop sends membership.deactivated, membership.went_invalid or membership.canceled. A cancel sent only as membership.updated is ignored (section 1; UNCONFIRMED whether production sends one).
2. Every other past due row: the customer stays limited until the past_due state is resolved.
3. A payment.failed that does not mark the row past_due (section 1): the row keeps the access it had, and the customer still gets the payment failed email.

If Twic's system caused the failure, Twic is responsible: we fix the cause and the customer should not lose paid access because of it. If Whop's system caused the failure, we report it to Whop support, and under Spencer's 26 October 2026 decision a customer who already has a paid account should keep access. The manual restore process for both cases is pending Spencer (see below). Until it exists, the code does not keep this promise: the same 7-day cap and limits in section 1 apply whatever the cause. This is policy intent, not current behavior, and nothing customer-facing should promise it yet.

Twic cannot currently tell those causes apart. The webhook sends every Whop payment.failed event to the same handler, handleWhopPaymentFailed (server.js:2897 through server.js:2898). The handler decides past_due only from billing_reason and the membership match (whopPaymentFailedMarksPastDue, lib/security.js:525 through lib/security.js:526), never from the cause, and writes it at server.js:2604. It reads the email, the manage link, billing_reason and the membership id. It does not read a failure reason.

A customer who reports a failure caused by Twic is handled by Twic support. A failure caused by Whop is reported to Whop support, and Twic stays the customer's point of contact until it is resolved.

There is no automatic detection of the cause. There is no automatic restored access beyond the Whop retry case. Any manual restore process is a separate proposal and is waiting on Spencer.

## 3. Known mismatches

These are open items. This document does not fix them. They stay as written until Spencer decides. Each states what is true on main 0b590b8684544ca64bd4fab2d7a17af0908a1d92.

1. The comment in userHasAccess now matches the rule (server.js:681 through server.js:682 on 0b590b86). The exact comment is: `past_due: pastDueAccess. A Whop retry keeps access for up to 7 days after its last Whop event; a Stripe only row keeps its old login and magic link result; every other past_due row fails closed.` This item is closed by the merge of PR #42.

2. The payment.failed log says access is kept up to 7 days while Whop retries (server.js:2605 on 0b590b86). The log text is: `marked ${email} past_due (access kept up to 7 days while Whop retries)`. That is true for the Whop retry case. It is not true for every other past due row, which stays limited, and the same line is logged for both.

3. The payment failed email tells the customer to update their payment method so access is not interrupted (server.js:2614). The exact line is: `Please update your payment method so your access isn't interrupted:`. That is true for the Whop retry case. It is not true for every other past due row, which is already limited when the email arrives. The same email includes an update payment link (server.js:2615). The app has no update payment link. The same email is also sent when the event did not mark the row past_due (item 8).

4. The Account panel. In the Whop retry case /api/me returns 200 and the panel shows the raw status text, past_due, with the trial end date when one is stored (public/index.html:7183 through public/index.html:7186 and public/index.html:5883 through public/index.html:5886). It does not say that a payment failed. For every other past due row /api/me returns 403 with only an error field, so the panel shows "Access revoked" (public/index.html:7186 and public/index.html:5886) rather than a payment message.

5. Stripe only past due: password login and the magic link issue a token, but the app refuses with 403 (section 1). This mismatch is unchanged from before.

6. KNOWN GAP: a Whop membership event that reports past_due or unresolved clears access (section 1, server.js:662).

7. Stripe to Whop movers are not yet covered (section 1).

8. A Whop payment.failed that is not a subscription_cycle renewal of the account's own stored membership that is not stale (another billing_reason, a null one, a stale event, another membership, or a failed Whop status update) leaves the row unchanged, so an active row keeps full access with no 7 day cap. It logs `payment.failed left ${email} unchanged (not a renewal of its stored membership, or stale)` and still sends the payment failed email whose subject begins "Action needed" (section 1; server.js:2606 through server.js:2611 and lib/security.js:525 through lib/security.js:526).

Item 1 is closed. Items 2 through 8 are held pending Spencer.
